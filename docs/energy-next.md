## Using the additions

The landing page links to `/demo/` and `/bg/demo/`. The simulation can switch between sunny, cloudy and outage scenarios, review a one-time night charge or held reserve, inspect each hour and reset. Its example prices and hardware are explicitly fictional; it makes no private API, permission or inverter request.

Inside **Controls → Energy assistant**, the preferences let a member enable sustained-energy observations and forecast-driven appliance replanning with current-password confirmation. Consumption checks require a full hour of excess over a robust historical baseline; solar checks require two hours of measured coverage and an earlier conservative daylight forecast. Missing readings and possible full-battery curtailment suppress advisories. An opening can notify once per episode and at most once per type in 24 hours, with no recovery message; the user can mark it seen or snooze it.

The appliance planner reserves existing tasks' power against the same solar surplus after household and battery needs. **Replan all future appliances** recomputes the whole schedule; already-started and already-reminded tasks retain their promised times. Missing forecasts leave saved plans visible. Old reminders are cancelled and timing, installation, data freshness and permission are checked again before dispatch. Appliances are always started manually.

**Away mode** accepts explicit departure, return and essential demand. Its preview compares normal and Away demand in the available forecast. Saving confirms the dates and versions with a password and preserves a normal-use model for absences longer than the rolling history window. That fallback is labelled as an estimate with extra uncertainty, and fresh normal readings replace it after return. The normal model excludes past absence readings, and matching future slots return to normal at the exact return boundary. Cancellation clips the historical period; an active hold and the physical battery/grid limits remain intact.

**Published power cuts** is opt-in. Load current public area names or enter a supply-point reference from your own bill. References are encrypted and never included unmasked in an archive or notification. Read-only checks use the fixed official portal, bounded responses and parsing workers, a shared cache and a global ceiling of 80 requests per rolling 24 hours. Failed checks remain unavailable. A published notice opens the outage calculator only after the member confirms that the property is affected; preparing the battery still uses the existing review and authorization flow. Disabling a watch removes its saved encrypted reference. The original portal remains available for verification.
